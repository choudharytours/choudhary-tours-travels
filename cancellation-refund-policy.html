Cancellation & Refund Policy

Please read our cancellation and refund policy carefully before making a booking.

Advance Payment

A booking is confirmed only after the agreed advance payment has been received by Choudhary Tours & Travels.

Cancellation by Customer

More than 72 hours before travel:
Up to 90% of the advance amount may be refunded after deducting applicable processing charges.

24 to 72 hours before travel:
Up to 50% of the advance amount may be refunded.

Less than 24 hours before travel:
No refund will be applicable.

No Show or After Journey Starts:
No refund will be provided once the journey has started or if the customer does not report for travel.

Cancellation by Company

If we are unable to provide the booked service due to unavoidable operational reasons and cannot arrange a suitable alternative, the refundable amount will be processed according to the applicable circumstances.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are generally processed within 7–10 business days through the original payment method, subject to banking timelines.

Non-Refundable Charges

Convenience fees, payment gateway charges and other non-recoverable third-party charges may be deducted from the refundable amount where applicable.

Force Majeure

Delays or cancellations caused by natural disasters, severe weather, road closures, government restrictions, strikes or other events beyond our reasonable control may affect travel services. Refunds or rescheduling in such situations will be handled on a case-by-case basis.
